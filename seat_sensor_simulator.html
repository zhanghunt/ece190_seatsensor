Power, clock and memory

Power cycle restarts setup() with millis() at 0. Without a coin cell the DS3231 resets to 2000-01-01 and reports lostPower(). Unplugging the clock makes rtc.now() return all-0xFF garbage, the way a failed I²C read does.
